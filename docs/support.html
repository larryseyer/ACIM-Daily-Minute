Support

Help for the ACIM Daily Minute apps and this website.

Contact

Email:

podcast@acimdailyminute.org

Typical response time: 1–2 business days.

When writing, include your device (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, or Apple Watch), the system version, the app version from Settings, and a short description of the issue.

Email support

What this covers

This page is the support contact for the ACIM Daily Minute apps on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Apple Watch, and for this website and the podcasts. Use it for app issues, general feedback, and feature requests.

The apps are free. There are no accounts, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no ads. See the apps.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reach a person?

Email podcast@acimdailyminute.org. That address is monitored. Please do not use social media profiles or App Store reviews as the first contact for a bug — email is the fastest way to get a reply.

Today’s passage or lesson is not loading

The apps fetch the daily reading from acimdailyminute.org. Check your connection, wait a moment, and open the app again. If it still fails, email us with your device, system version, and app version.

How do I change or turn off reminders?

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Open the app’s Settings to change the Daily Minute time, the Daily Lesson time, or the practice reminders, or to turn them off. Nothing about your schedule is sent anywhere.

Where are my highlights, notes, and bookmarks?

They stay on your device by default. If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, they travel between your own Apple devices through your personal iCloud private database. That database is not readable by us. iCloud sync is off unless you turn it on.

The Apple Watch reading is stale

Open the iPhone app while the Watch is nearby, then reopen the Watch app. If a complication is blank, re-add it from the Watch face editor.

A Home Screen or Mac widget is not updating

Widgets refresh on Apple’s schedule. Tap the widget to open the app, which forces a fresh fetch. If it stays empty, remove the widget and add it again from the widget gallery.

Are there in-app purchases or subscriptions?

No. The apps are free, with no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no restore-purchases flow. If you see an unexpected App Store charge, use Apple’s Report a Problem page.

How do I delete my data or an account?

There are no user accounts. Uninstalling the app removes on-device data (highlights, notes, bookmarks, and reminder times). If you turned on iCloud sync, you can turn it off in Settings, or delete the app’s data from iCloud in your device’s Apple Account settings. We cannot see or delete that iCloud data for you.

What data do you collect?

None. No analytics, no crash reporters, no advertising, and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

The website or podcast is the issue, not the app

Email the same address: podcast@acimdailyminute.org. Include the page URL or the podcast episode date.

Privacy

The privacy policy for the website and the apps is at acimdailyminute.org/privacy.html.

Donations

The apps and this site are free. If you want to contribute to operating costs, that is separate from the App Store listing and is handled on the Donations page.
