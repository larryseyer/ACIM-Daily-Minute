Privacy Policy

Applies to the ACIM Daily Minute website and the ACIM Daily Minute apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Apple Watch.

ACIM Daily Minute collects no data whatsoever. No analytics, no crash reporters, no ad networks, no user accounts, and no tracking of any kind.

The apps

The apps contain zero third-party SDKs — only Apple's built-in frameworks. There are no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no user accounts, and no device fingerprinting.

The apps make network requests only to acimdailyminute.org, to fetch the daily passage, the Workbook lessons, and the podcast feeds. Cached passages never leave your device.

Highlights, notes, and bookmarks

Your highlights, notes, and bookmarks stay on your device by default. If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, they travel between your own Apple devices through your personal iCloud private database. That database is tied to your Apple Account and is not readable by the developer. iCloud sync is off unless you turn it on.

Reminders and notifications

All reminders — the Daily Minute reminder, the Daily Lesson reminder, and the practice reminders that follow the Workbook lesson you are on — are scheduled entirely on your device. Nothing about your schedule is sent anywhere.

The website

The website serves static pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts of its own.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may record standard server access logs (such as IP address, browser type, and requested page) in the course of delivering the site. ACIM Daily Minute does not receive, store, or analyze that information.

Some pages link to or embed third-party services — YouTube for video, and podcast directories for audio subscriptions. When you follow one of those links or play an embedded video, that service's own privacy policy applies to what it collects.

Children

ACIM Daily Minute is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

App Store privacy label

Data Not Collected. ACIM Daily Minute does not collect any data from users. Optional iCloud sync stores your highlights, notes, and bookmarks in your own iCloud private database; the developer cannot read it, so it is not data collected by the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the Support page.
