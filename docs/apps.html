The App

A Course in Miracles, on every screen you own

The Daily Minute reader, built natively for Apple platforms — the same passages and lessons you follow here, waiting when you open the app.

Coming to the App Store — September 13, 2026

Download links will appear here on launch day.

Open the app and today's minute is waiting — a short passage from A Course in Miracles paired with that day's Workbook Lesson, refreshed automatically. Every passage is drawn from the Sparkly Edition and presented exactly as written, without commentary. One minute, one lesson, every day.

One reader, five devices

iPhone & iPad

Today's passage, the full Workbook, the searchable archive, saved highlights, and audio — in one reader.

Mac

The same reader on the desktop, plus small, medium, and large widgets showing today's minute.

Apple TV

Today's reading as a player on the television.

Apple Watch

Today's minute on your wrist, with three complication styles and phone-to-watch sync.

What's inside

  • Today — today's daily minute and Workbook Lesson, refreshed automatically.
  • Read — the Workbook spine and the Text; search by number or keyword, jump to any lesson.
  • Listen — podcast feeds for daily minutes and lessons, plus embedded YouTube audio.
  • Archive — a calendar of every past daily minute, searchable by date or text.
  • Saved — bookmark any passage; swipe to remove.
  • Reminders — a Daily Minute and a Daily Lesson reminder at your own times, plus practice reminders that follow the lesson you're on, with a custom chime.
  • Widgets — small, medium, and large Home Screen and Mac widgets.
  • Live Activity — Dynamic Island and Lock Screen presence while today's minute is active.
  • Siri — “What's today's reading?”

Private by design

No data collected. No analytics, no crash reporters, no ad networks, no user accounts, and no tracking of any kind. Zero third-party SDKs — only Apple's built-in frameworks. Network requests go only to acimdailyminute.org to fetch the daily content.

Optional iCloud sync keeps your highlights, notes, and bookmarks in your own private iCloud database — off unless you turn it on, and unreadable to us. Read the full privacy policy.

Other platforms

Windows and Linux versions are planned to follow. Android is under evaluation.

Every passage is the Sparkly Edition of A Course in Miracles, based on the 1972 manuscript edited by William Thetford with Helen Schucman and distributed copyright-free. The app is an independent companion and does not represent or claim affiliation with any organization.
